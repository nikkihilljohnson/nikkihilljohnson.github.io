# Website Strategy

## Primary Goal
Generate consulting and freelance marketing opportunities.

## Secondary Goal
Serve as a portfolio for potential employers.

## Target Audience

### Primary
Small to mid-sized businesses needing social media strategy, content strategy, paid social, analytics, and reporting.

### Secondary
Marketing leaders and recruiters evaluating Nikki for senior marketing roles.

## Positioning

Nikki bridges strategy, creativity, and analytics.

She understands how to create content people care about and how to measure whether it actually works.

## Core Message

Creating content is easy.
Creating results is harder.
