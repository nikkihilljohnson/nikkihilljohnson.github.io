About

Elder millennial. Oregon Trail survivor.

I was making videos with a handheld camcorder in my basement before I even had internet. Turns out I've been making content my whole life. Twenty years and approximately a million algorithm changes later, I'm still finding new ways to get people to pay attention.

The short version.

I have a sociology degree, which explains a lot. I've always been more interested in why people do things than which app they're doing them on.

I started at AOL in 2006, working in broadband marketing before moving into music and lifestyle content. At night, I was at the Connecticut School of Broadcasting, training in TV and radio. I graduated Best in Radio in my class.

Then I spent eight years at BODi building an online fitness community of more than 1,000 people. That's where I learned what makes people show up, stick around, and actually care. Everything since has been some version of that: nonprofit marketing at Carolina Farm Trust, a $6.4 million launch with James Wedmore, and two years turning social media into a sales channel at HVAC.com.

In late 2025, I went out on my own. Now I work with brands that need experienced marketing leadership and Democratic candidates who need someone who understands both the message and how to get it in front of people.

I live in the Charleston area with my husband, four kids, three cats, and a dog. Organized chaos is my natural habitat.
