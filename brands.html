Brands

Boring categories are my favorite.

Air purifiers. HVAC. Countertops. Online education. Fitness. I've worked across industries that don't have much in common, except that they all need to give people a reason to pay attention.

Sometimes that means making a complicated product easier to understand. Sometimes it's finding the humor in something ordinary. And sometimes it's building a community around something people genuinely care about.

Organic gives people a reason to care. Paid makes sure more of them see it. You need both.

No one cares about their HVAC until it breaks.

Senior Social Media & Content Strategist: organic, paid, and on-camera content.

Usually in July. Nobody's scrolling Instagram thinking about their HVAC system. They're thinking about staying cool, keeping their family comfortable, and avoiding a very expensive surprise. So that's what we talked about.

I joined HVAC.com when it was still a startup and stayed through its acquisition by Trane Technologies. I built the social voice and paid program from the ground up, mixing seasonal content, memes, and educational videos with Meta campaigns designed to turn social engagement into measurable website conversions. I was also the face of the account, filming dozens of videos that made HVAC a little less boring.

Social became a sales channel. Meta advertising accounted for approximately 6% of total company sales.

James Wedmore· Business by Design2022–2023

The audience was there. They were just scrolling past.

Creative Content Director: Instagram, YouTube Shorts, and launch coverage.

James already had an audience of more than 100K Instagram followers, but the content wasn't getting the attention it could. Ahead of a major launch, we leaned into his personality with pop culture references, stronger opening hooks, and more short-form video. We were producing 50+ creative assets a week without losing what made his content feel like him.

I repurposed our best-performing content for YouTube Shorts to reach new audiences and managed live social coverage throughout launch week.

Reach up 540% in 90 days. Stronger hooks and more personality helped turn an established audience into an engaged one.

Not Your Average Grandma· Personal brandBrand rebuild

Great voice. Messy grid.

Brand rebuild, visual system, and short-form video.

Not Your Average Grandma already had a strong message for women navigating midlife. But the Instagram account didn't reflect it. The colors, fonts, and content were all over the place, and there wasn't a consistent posting strategy.

I audited the account, built a cohesive visual identity, and developed three content pillars. Then I turned her podcast into a repeatable short-form video series, testing different hooks to see what actually got people watching.

The voice was already there. The strategy and visuals just needed to catch up.
