Ann Shippy· SC House District 123Client

Four weeks out. 128,000 accounts reached.

Meta ads: strategy, graphics, video, and copy.

Ann brought me in with a month left before Election Day. The goal was awareness: put Ann and her issues in front of as many people in Hilton Head, Daufuskie, and Levy as the budget allowed.

The creative covered her introduction, a Jaime Harrison endorsement video, a contrast ad, and issue ads on coastal growth, data centers, and gerrymandering. I launched a first round, then added new rounds and increased the budget as results came in. The coastal growth ad, “Protect What Makes This Place Home,” proved the most efficient and reached 93,488 accounts on its own.

Ann was clear from day one that her voters skewed older and retired. The data backed her up: people 65 and older engaged 6.6 times more than 18 to 24 year olds, and engagement rose with every age group. The endorsement video held attention best, watched at up to 18 times the rate of the other videos. Costs held steady through the final stretch, finishing at $5.55 per 1,000 impressions.

Katie Miller· Charleston County CouncilClient

Katie did the research. I got people to share it.

Creator collaboration and video editing.

Katie had been digging into whether a massive AI data center was coming to Charleston. I made a Nikki Next Door video about what she found and posted it as a collaboration with her account, so it reached both audiences at once.

People passed it along: 74 shares and 49 reposts. Three out of four viewers didn’t already follow me, and Instagram rated every engagement rate on the video above my usual.

For Katie’s own account, I cut two of her longer recordings into four short clips, each built around one message. One was a direct appeal to Republican voters, “Republican? Hear me out,” which drew 2,200+ views and 91 likes across Instagram and Facebook. East Cooper Dems, Peninsula Dems, and another South Carolina political creator reposted it.
