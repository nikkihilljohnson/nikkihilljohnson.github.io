The Challenge

Build awareness and generate qualified leads in a category most homeowners actively ignore until something breaks. The challenge wasn't visibility — it was relevance. HVAC isn't aspirational, but comfort, safety, and a healthy home absolutely are.

The goal was to reach homeowners long before urgency forced the conversation.

The Insight

Homeowners care deeply about their homes. They rarely care about HVAC equipment.

The strategy pivoted from selling hardware to selling what HVAC actually represents: comfort, safety, air quality, and peace of mind. That shift changed how people engaged with the brand — and how they converted.

The Strategy

Built a full-funnel organic and paid social ecosystem designed to earn trust first and capture demand when it surfaced. Established HVAC.com's social voice and paid media foundation from the ground up — led with education, empathy, and homeowner-first storytelling instead of product features.

Content

  • Seasonal content mapped to real homeowner stress points — summer heat, winter prep, allergy season
  • Short-form video centered on relatable "this just happened" homeowner moments
  • Educational carousels simplifying air quality, maintenance, and energy efficiency
  • Strategic meme usage to humanize the brand and increase organic share velocity
  • Social proof styled like native community conversations to build credibility

Paid Media

  • Structured a Meta ecosystem to balance education, awareness, and conversion readiness
  • Continuous creative testing across hooks, formats, and audience segments
  • Retargeting built from behavioral signals: site visitors, video viewers, engaged audiences
  • Weekly CPL and ROAS monitoring with monthly cohort optimization cycles
  • Intentional alignment between organic storytelling and paid amplification

The Challenge

StoneSmith didn't just need better social. They needed social. The goal was to launch from zero, build local brand recognition, and create a visual presence that showcased craftsmanship while still feeling approachable to homeowners.

The Insight

Countertop brands tend to go one of two directions: luxury showroom vibes that feel a little out of reach, or contractor content that's technical but not inspiring.

StoneSmith needed a middle ground — beautiful work presented in a way that felt welcoming, not exclusive. That balance became the north star.

The Strategy

Built the brand's social foundation from the ground up — strategically, visually, and structurally. Established a cohesive visual identity that reflected craftsmanship and warmth, then built a content system designed to guide homeowners from inspiration to inquiry.

Content Pillars

  • Project Showcases: Before-and-after transformations, installation highlights, finished kitchens and bathrooms
  • Education: Material comparisons, care guides, and design trend breakdowns that simplified decision-making
  • Lifestyle: Aspirational home content placing StoneSmith within real, livable spaces

Foundation Work

The Challenge

Transform a strong personal voice into a cohesive, visually recognizable brand — increasing followers, engagement, and short-form discoverability across platforms.

The goal wasn't to change her message. It was to package it in a way that honored both honesty and aspiration for a midlife female audience.

The Insight

The voice was strong. The visuals were not.

Inconsistent colors, mixed typography, and an undefined posting rhythm created friction for new followers. The message resonated, but the presentation diluted discoverability and authority. The brand needed alignment between substance and aesthetic.

The Strategy

Rebuilt the brand around clarity, consistency, and strategic repurposing — centered on authenticity, storytelling, and visual cohesion.

Brand Rebuild

  • Conducted a full content audit to separate signal from noise
  • Defined 3–4 core content pillars aligned with audience pain points and aspirations
  • Designed a cohesive visual system: typography, color palette, graphic templates
  • Established a consistent posting rhythm with intentional variety across pillars

Short-Form Video

  • Identified high-impact podcast moments with standalone value for Reels
  • Built a repeatable Reels framework: hook → insight → takeaway → subtle CTA
  • Batch-produced weekly video content to maintain consistency
  • Tested multiple hook styles to determine what stopped the scroll

Instead of creating more content, we amplified what already worked.

The Challenge

Support Maple Air's marketing evolution by bringing the brand to life on social, strengthening positioning, preparing for ecommerce, and building demand in the indoor air quality category.

The brand already existed. The challenge was making it visible, relatable, and strategically positioned for health-conscious homeowners.

The Insight

Nobody wakes up excited to shop for air purification. Usually it starts with allergies, kids getting sick, or a "why does the house smell weird" moment.

The strategy wasn't to scare people. It was to make clean air feel practical, relevant, and worth thinking about before there's a problem.

The Strategy

Acting as fractional marketing lead, I'm focused on aligning brand, content, and growth strategy while supporting a broader marketing evolution.

Content

  • Established Maple Air's social voice and platform presence from an inconsistent starting point
  • Educational posts on indoor air quality, allergens, and whole-home health
  • Install and behind-the-scenes content that humanizes the technology
  • Seasonal awareness tied to health conversations: flu season, mold concerns, air quality events
  • Lifestyle storytelling connecting clean air to sleep, wellness, and family comfort

Growth & Brand

  • Supporting brand refresh initiatives, website direction, and product storytelling
  • Collaborating with animation partners to simplify complex technology for consumers
  • Leading influencer outreach strategy to normalize conversations around air quality
  • Aligning organic social with future paid media and ecommerce goals
