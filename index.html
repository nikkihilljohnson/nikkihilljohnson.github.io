So I start with the person on the other side of the screen: what they're worried about, what they'd forward to a friend, what would make them stop scrolling. Then I let the numbers check my work.

How I build a paid campaign
  1. Start with the voter file.

    Known voters get the repetition. Everyone else in the area gets low-cost awareness.

  2. Never pay twice.

    Audiences stay separate, so the budget doesn't buy the same person in two places.

  3. Test creative in rounds.

    Several messages run side by side. Delivery shows which one voters respond to, and the next round builds on it.

  4. Report every week.

    What ran, what it cost, who saw it, and what changes next.

A school board race, run like a pro campaign.

Everything digital: strategy, graphics, video, copy and Meta ads.

Two audiences that never overlap. Known District 1 voters from the voter file saw the ads about nine times each. Everyone else in the target area got low-cost awareness, with the voter list excluded so no one got paid for twice. Creative ran in rounds, and every version was tested in both audiences.

The voter file paid for itself. Known voters were more than eight times as likely to watch 15 seconds or more as the general audience.
